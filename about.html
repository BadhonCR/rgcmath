About the College

With the nationalization of Carmichael College on 01 January 1963, opportunities for higher education became limited in Rangpur. To meet the growing demand of students, Professor Emaz Uddin and many distinguished citizens took the initiative to establish another higher educational institution in Rangpur.

Their combined efforts and generous donations of land and financial support resulted in the establishment of Rangpur Government College on 25 July 1963, which has since become one of the leading institutions of higher education in northern Bangladesh.

Initially, the college operated as a night college at Koilash Ranjan High School and as a day college at the historic Zamindarbari (Kalidham) of Ram Babu. Professor Emaz Uddin served as the founding Principal, while Professor Fulae Hossain became the first Vice Principal of the institution.

The college was affiliated with the University of Rajshahi from its establishment until 1992. At the beginning, permission was granted to open only the Intermediate Arts (IA) programme. Due to the excellent academic performance of the institution, Bachelor of Arts (BA) courses were introduced in 1965, followed by Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) programmes in 1970.

The college moved to its permanent campus in 1967. A boys' hostel was established at the former Kalidham campus to provide accommodation for students from different districts of Bangladesh.

During the Great Liberation War of 1971, the teachers, students and people of Rangpur actively participated in the struggle for independence. One of the students of the college, Moslem Uddin, embraced martyrdom for the country. In honour of his supreme sacrifice, the boys' hostel was later renamed Shaheed Moslem Uddin Hall.

Rangpur Government College was officially nationalized on 01 November 1984. After nationalization, student enrolment increased rapidly, leading to the construction of new academic buildings, laboratories, libraries and student hostels. In 1993, the college became affiliated with the National University of Bangladesh.

Today the college offers Higher Secondary, Degree (Pass), Honours and Master's programmes in various disciplines. Student organizations such as BNCC, Rover Scout and Bandhon (Voluntary Blood Donors' Association) continue to provide leadership, discipline and humanitarian services to the students.

Department of Mathematics

The Department of Mathematics started its undergraduate Honours programme in 2011 together with the Departments of English, Botany and Zoology.

Since its establishment, the department has been committed to providing quality education in Pure Mathematics, Applied Mathematics and related disciplines under the National University of Bangladesh.

The department aims to develop analytical thinking, problem-solving skills and research interest among students through modern teaching methods, seminars and academic activities.
