The department provides quality education through experienced faculty members, modern teaching methods and academic activities that encourage analytical thinking, logical reasoning and research.

HEAD'S MESSAGE

Message from the Department Head

Welcome to the Department of Mathematics, Rangpur Government College.

Welcome to Our Department

Mathematics is not merely a subject; it is the foundation of scientific thinking, innovation and technological advancement. Our department is dedicated to providing quality education through experienced faculty members, modern teaching methods and a friendly academic environment.

We encourage our students to develop logical reasoning, critical thinking, creativity and research skills so that they become capable of contributing meaningfully to society and the nation.

I warmly invite all students, guardians and visitors to explore our department and become a part of our academic journey.

Masuma Akhter
Head of the Department
